==============================================================================

OVERALL STATUS: PASS

PURPOSE
-------
Stage 20 establishes naive baselines for the three-class Fire_Risk_Label target before class-imbalance handling or real classifiers are introduced. The baselines are Dummy_Most_Frequent and Dummy_Stratified.

TARGET SEMANTICS
----------------
The target classes are Normal, Warning, and Critical. The source labels represent hourly keeper/expert fire-risk assessments carried forward across the associated 10-minute sensor rows. The operational classifier will produce a prediction every 10 minutes, so primary classification metrics are reported at row level. Unique hourly assessment counts are separately documented to avoid treating every 10-minute row as an independent keeper decision.

WHY ACCURACY IS NOT ENOUGH
--------------------------
The majority class in Train is Normal, representing approximately 96.3068% of Train rows. A Most-Frequent classifier can therefore obtain high raw Accuracy while producing zero recall for Warning and Critical. For this research task, Balanced Accuracy, Macro-F1, and especially Warning/Critical recall are more informative than Accuracy alone.
