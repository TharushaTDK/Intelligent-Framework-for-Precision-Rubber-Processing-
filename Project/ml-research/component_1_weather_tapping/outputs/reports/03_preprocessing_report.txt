Preprocessing applied to a copy of the prepared farmer dataset (stage 00):
- invalid negative rainfall set to missing
- plausible physical bounds checked for pH, humidity, soil moisture, temperature, DRC
- categorical whitespace/case normalized
- month/day-of-year features extracted
- rainfall-rule tapping proxy target derived (no added noise)
- missing values are imputed inside model pipelines to avoid train/test leakage
- raw workbook remains unchanged
Warning: DRC values were normalized by the dataset author and tapping labels are rule-derived;
neither is verified field ground truth.
