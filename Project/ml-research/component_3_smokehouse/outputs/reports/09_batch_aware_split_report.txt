METHODOLOGY
-----------
The 24,463 rows are repeated 10-minute observations nested inside 12 complete drying batches. Therefore individual rows are not treated as independent units for splitting. Complete Smokehouse_ID + Batch_ID groups are assigned to Train, Validation, or Test.

A purely chronological 8/2/2 batch split was rejected because the corresponding B05 validation batches contain no Warning/Critical hourly assessment events. A fixed group-stratified split is therefore used to retain both smokehouses, all three layers, and all three fire-risk classes in every split while keeping whole batches isolated.

The TEST split is now frozen and must not be used for model selection, hyperparameter tuning, preprocessing fitting, class-weight decisions, feature decisions, or threshold decisions.
