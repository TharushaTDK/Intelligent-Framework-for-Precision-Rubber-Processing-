Stage 32 explainability
========================
Permutation importance was computed on the held-out test partition for the selected models.
Impurity importance is reported for tree-based selected models.
SHAP was NOT used (not installed); correlation and importance do not prove causation.
DRC values were normalized by the dataset author, price is a formula of DRC and month, and
tapping labels come from a rainfall rule, so importances describe those constructions, not
verified agronomy.
