EDA INTERPRETATION NOTES
------------------------
- L1 is generally warmer and less humid than L2/L3, while L3 shows the longest average sequence-level drying duration.
- Across the 36 sequences, higher mean temperature is associated with shorter total drying time, while higher mean humidity is associated with longer total drying time.
- Remaining_Drying_Time is strongly associated with Elapsed_Drying_Hours by construction, but elapsed time alone is not treated as sufficient evidence of final model performance.
- Fire_Risk_Label is strongly imbalanced. Accuracy alone will therefore be unsuitable for later model evaluation.
- Warning and Critical assessment conditions must not be interpreted using a single temperature or humidity threshold; later temporal analysis will examine recent behavior and trends.
- Actual_Total_Drying_Time is inspected descriptively only and remains excluded from deployed predictors.
- Stage 06 will analyze time dependence, layer-wise trajectories and temporal behavior. No rolling or lag features are created in Stage 05.
