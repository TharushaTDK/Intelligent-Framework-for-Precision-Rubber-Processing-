WEIGHT FORMULA
--------------
Balanced weight for class c:
    n_samples / (n_classes * n_samples_c)

Weights are computed from Train only. Validation and Test must not influence class-weight calculation.

WHY CLASS WEIGHTING IS SELECTED
-------------------------------
The Train data are strongly imbalanced, especially for the Critical class. However, the observations are grouped and temporal: only 8 complete batches form Train, and fire-risk labels originate from hourly keeper/expert assessments that are carried across 10-minute sensor rows. Synthetic oversampling such as SMOTE could generate artificial feature combinations that do not correspond to valid smokehouse temporal states. Therefore class/sample weighting is selected as the primary imbalance strategy.

MODEL-SPECIFIC STRATEGY
-----------------------
Logistic Regression: class_weight='balanced'
Random Forest: class_weight='balanced'
Extra Trees: class_weight='balanced'
Gradient Boosting: TRAIN-derived sample_weight
XGBoost: TRAIN-derived sample_weight
SMOTE / synthetic oversampling: not used

EVALUATION PRIORITY
-------------------
Primary comparison metric: Macro-F1.
Also important: Balanced Accuracy.
Safety-sensitive metrics: Warning Recall and Critical Recall.
Accuracy is retained only as a supporting metric because the Normal class dominates the dataset.
